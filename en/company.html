ABOUT IPEUN

Needle-free technology,
new possibilities

IPEUN develops medical and aesthetic devices based on precision needle-free jet technology. Our All-Jet medical device and INVERA aesthetic device are designed for different skin care settings.

With in-house research, development and design capabilities, we consider both technical precision and ease of use to expand the possibilities of needle-free technology.
