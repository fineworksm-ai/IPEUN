What is All-Jet

Needle-free injection and RF,
in one platform

All-Jet uses solenoid-based ultra-high-pressure energy to deliver medication as a microjet. Select a 150, 200 or 250 μm nozzle according to drug viscosity and use the integrated 40.68 MHz radiofrequency handpiece. The dual cooling system is designed to reduce cooling-related waiting and support continuous operation.

01

Needle-free injection

Deliver medication into the skin without needles

02

Fast, continuous procedures

Work efficiently over large areas without waiting for cooling

03

Selectable nozzles

Choose from three nozzles to suit drug viscosity

RF handpiece

40.68 MHz radiofrequency

KEY TECHNOLOGY

All-Jet’s core technology

01, PRECISION NOZZLE

Energy-focusing nozzle

Fluid-dynamics-based downward taper, Minimized vortices, Three interchangeable nozzles (150/200/250 μm)

Compatible across drug viscosities

02, RADIO FREQUENCY

RF handpiece

A 40.68 MHz radiofrequency handpiece for All-Jet

Improved drug delivery efficiency

03, DUAL COOLING

Dual cooling system

Primary solenoid immersion cooling, Secondary cooling with four heat pipes and a Peltier element, Maintains 40°C or below

Over one hour of continuous operation

How microjet delivery works
