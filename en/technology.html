OUR TECHNOLOGY

Two products,
one needle-free technology.

Solenoid-based precision microjet technology.

SHARED TECHNOLOGY

Precision and user experience,
designed together.

Our solenoid-based precision microjet technology combines an energy-focusing nozzle with cooling designed for continuous use. It forms the foundation of two products for medical and aesthetic settings.

All-Jet and INVERA both feature an energy-focusing nozzle and dual cooling system. All-Jet also includes a 40.68 MHz radiofrequency handpiece.

CORE DESIGN

Three core designs

01 · PRECISION NOZZLE

Energy-focusing nozzle

Fluid-dynamics-based downward taper · Minimized vortices · Three interchangeable nozzles (150/200/250 μm)

All-Jet · Compatible across drug viscositiesPatent applications →

02 · RADIO FREQUENCY

RF handpiece (All-Jet)

A 40.68 MHz radiofrequency handpiece for All-Jet

Improved drug delivery efficiencyPatent applications →

03 · DUAL COOLING

Dual cooling system

Primary solenoid immersion cooling · Secondary cooling with four heat pipes and a Peltier element · Maintains 40°C or below

Over one hour of continuous operationPatent applications →

OPERATING PRINCIPLE

How microjet delivery works

STEP 01

Instant jet delivery

Solenoid-based high-pressure energy delivers a brief jet of medication.

STEP 02

Delivery into the skin

The medication sprayed as a microjet is delivered into the skin layers.

STEP 03

Even delivery

The focusing nozzle is designed to support stable delivery of active ingredients.

STEP 04

Broader applications
